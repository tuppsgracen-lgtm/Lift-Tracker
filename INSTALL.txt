Daily Lift v7

The ZIP contains the updated app files. To update your existing installation,
replace its files with these files at the SAME address. Your device-local
dailyLiftState.v1 data migrates automatically. Keep a copy of your old files.

For a fresh installation, open the published link in Safari, then use
Share > Add to Home Screen.

A new address stores data separately from an old address. Workouts do not
automatically transfer between browsers, addresses, or devices. Use Settings
> Export Backup / Restore when a Daily Lift JSON backup is available.

Warm-up percentages use a recorded max from Best Efforts > Personal Bests.
Optional percentages are configured beside the planned reps in the editor.
Weights round to the nearest 5 lb. You can adjust them before completing a set.
Hold the weight buttons for repeated 5 lb changes.

Personal Bests shows actual weights. Only the graph estimates 1RM for bench,
back squat, and front squat. Power clean progression uses actual TS weights.
